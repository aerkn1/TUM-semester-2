## Course Procedure And Online Etiquette

The general information file states that some Language Center courses take place online and require extra course-procedure rules. Practical implications:

- Join from a quiet room where online work is possible without disturbance.
- Microphone and camera are considered necessary for communicative language teaching.
- Test technical equipment before class.
- If transmission problems occur, disconnecting VPN and closing other server-connected programs may help.
- Audio and video recordings of synchronous courses are not allowed.
- Online language teaching requires additional time commitment and more learner autonomy than a presence-based course.
- Active and concentrated classroom participation is expected.
