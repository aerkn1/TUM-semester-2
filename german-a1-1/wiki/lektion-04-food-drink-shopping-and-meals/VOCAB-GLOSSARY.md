## Level Summary

- Main output domain: food, drink, grocery shopping, ordering, and meals.
- Must-produce items: common nouns with articles, `ich möchte`, `ich nehme`, `mögen`, quantity phrases, `Was kostet das?`, `essen`, `trinken`, meal phrases, yes/no questions, and direct-object noun phrases.
- Main trap: direct translation from English into German customer language; use short A1 patterns, check masculine accusative objects, and separate `kein` from `nicht gern`.

## Food Nouns

### das Brot
- Meaning: bread.
- Plural: `die Brote` for loaves/types; often used as mass noun.
- Example: `Ich kaufe Brot.`
- Pitfall: do not force plural for ordinary bread as food.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "I buy bread."
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### das Brötchen
- Meaning: bread roll.
- Plural: `die Brötchen`.
- Example: `Ich möchte zwei Brötchen.`
- Pitfall: same form in singular and plural.
- Flashcard prompt: Order two bread rolls.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### der Käse
- Meaning: cheese.
- Plural: `die Käse` for types; often mass noun.
- Example: `Ich möchte Käse.`
- Pitfall: masculine article: `der Käse`.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask for cheese.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### die Wurst
- Meaning: sausage/cold cut.
- Plural: `die Würste`.
- Example: `Ich esse Wurst.`
- Pitfall: umlaut in plural.
- Flashcard prompt: Say that you eat sausage.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### das Ei
- Meaning: egg.
- Plural: `die Eier`.
- Example: `Ich kaufe sechs Eier.`
- Pitfall: plural changes strongly: `Ei` -> `Eier`.
- Flashcard prompt: Buy six eggs.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### der Apfel
- Meaning: apple.
- Plural: `die Äpfel`.
- Example: `Ich möchte einen Apfel.`
- Pitfall: accusative after `ich möchte`: `einen Apfel`.
- Flashcard prompt: Order one apple.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### die Banane
- Meaning: banana.
- Plural: `die Bananen`.
- Example: `Ich möchte eine Banane.`
- Pitfall: feminine article: `eine Banane`.
- Flashcard prompt: Order one banana.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### die Kartoffel
- Meaning: potato.
- Plural: `die Kartoffeln`.
- Example: `Ich brauche ein Kilo Kartoffeln.`
- Pitfall: commonly used with quantity/weight.
- Flashcard prompt: Buy one kilo of potatoes.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### der Reis
- Meaning: rice.
- Plural: usually mass noun in A1 food context.
- Example: `Ich esse Reis.`
- Pitfall: no article needed in a simple food statement.
- Flashcard prompt: Say that you eat rice.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### die Nudeln
- Meaning: noodles/pasta.
- Plural: plural form.
- Example: `Ich esse Nudeln.`
- Pitfall: often plural in ordinary food use.
- Flashcard prompt: Say that you eat pasta.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

## Drink Nouns

### das Wasser
- Meaning: water.
- Plural: `die Wasser` only for types; normally mass noun.
- Example: `Ich möchte ein Wasser.`
- Pitfall: in ordering, `ein Wasser` can mean one glass/bottle of water.
- Flashcard prompt: Order a water.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### der Kaffee
- Meaning: coffee.
- Plural: `die Kaffees` for cups/types.
- Example: `Ich trinke Kaffee.`
- Pitfall: customer request often uses accusative: `einen Kaffee`.
- Flashcard prompt: Order a coffee.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### der Tee
- Meaning: tea.
- Plural: `die Tees` for types/cups.
- Example: `Ich möchte einen Tee.`
- Pitfall: masculine article: `der Tee`.
- Flashcard prompt: Order a tea.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### die Milch
- Meaning: milk.
- Plural: usually mass noun.
- Example: `Ich brauche Milch.`
- Pitfall: feminine article, but often no article in shopping-list style.
- Flashcard prompt: Say you need milk.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### der Saft
- Meaning: juice.
- Plural: `die Säfte`.
- Example: `Ich möchte ein Glas Saft.`
- Pitfall: umlaut in plural.
- Flashcard prompt: Order a glass of juice.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### das Bier
- Meaning: beer.
- Plural: `die Biere`.
- Example: `Ich trinke kein Bier.`
- Pitfall: neuter article: `das Bier`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say that you do not drink beer.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

## Shopping And Ordering Verbs

### möchten
- Meaning: would like.
- Forms: `ich möchte`, `du möchtest`, `er/sie möchte`, `Sie möchten`.
- Example: `Ich möchte ein Brot.`
- Pitfall: much safer and more polite than `ich will` in customer dialogue; masculine objects change: `einen Kaffee`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "I would like a bread roll."
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### nehmen
- Meaning: to take; in restaurant/shop language, to choose/order.
- Forms: `ich nehme`, `du nimmst`, `er/sie nimmt`, `Sie nehmen`.
- Example: `Ich nehme die Suppe und einen Kaffee.`
- Pitfall: stem change in `du nimmst`, `er/sie nimmt`; masculine objects take `einen`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "I will take the soup and a coffee."
- CEFR note: enriched from official/trusted A1 grammar sequence.

### mögen
- Meaning: to like.
- Forms: `ich mag`, `du magst`, `er/sie mag`, `Sie mögen`.
- Example: `Ich mag Kaffee, aber ich mag keinen Fisch.`
- Pitfall: not the same as `möchten`; `mögen` = like generally, `möchten` = would like now.
- Flashcard prompt: Say one food you like and one you do not like.
- CEFR note: enriched from official/trusted A1 grammar sequence.

### kaufen
- Meaning: to buy.
- Forms: `ich kaufe`, `du kaufst`, `er/sie kauft`, `Sie kaufen`.
- Example: `Ich kaufe Äpfel.`
- Pitfall: use for purchase, not eating.
- Flashcard prompt: Say you buy apples.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### brauchen
- Meaning: to need.
- Forms: `ich brauche`, `du brauchst`, `er/sie braucht`, `Sie brauchen`.
- Example: `Ich brauche Milch und Eier.`
- Pitfall: `brauchen` takes a noun/object; do not use as a modal here.
- Flashcard prompt: Say two things you need.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### kosten
- Meaning: to cost.
- Forms: `es kostet`, `sie kosten`.
- Example: `Was kostet das?`
- Pitfall: include the thing that costs: `das`, `der Käse`, `die Äpfel`.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask what something costs.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### essen
- Meaning: to eat.
- Forms: `ich esse`, `du isst`, `er/sie isst`, `Sie essen`.
- Example: `Ich esse Brot.`
- Pitfall: stem change in `du isst`, `er/sie isst`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say what you eat for breakfast.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### trinken
- Meaning: to drink.
- Forms: `ich trinke`, `du trinkst`, `er/sie trinkt`, `Sie trinken`.
- Example: `Ich trinke Wasser.`
- Pitfall: use only for drinks.
- Flashcard prompt: Say what you drink.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### schmecken
- Meaning: to taste.
- Forms: `es schmeckt`, `sie schmecken`.
- Example: `Es schmeckt gut.`
- Pitfall: the food is the subject; do not translate English "I taste it" directly.
- Flashcard prompt: Say the food tastes good.
- CEFR note: enriched A1.1.

### sehen
- Meaning: to see.
- Forms: `ich sehe`, `du siehst`, `er/sie sieht`, `Sie sehen`.
- Example: `Ich sehe einen langen Zug.`
- Pitfall: the thing/person seen is an accusative object; masculine article and adjective change.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "I see a long train."
- CEFR note: local/enriched A1.1 from the accusative-adjective handout.

## Accusative Object Mini-Table

| Base Noun | Request/Order Form | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `der Kaffee` | `Ich möchte einen Kaffee.` | masculine direct object |
| `der Tee` | `Ich nehme einen Tee.` | masculine direct object |
| `der Apfel` | `Ich möchte einen Apfel.` | masculine direct object |
| `der Fisch` | `Ich mag keinen Fisch.` | masculine negative direct object |
| `das Wasser` | `Ich möchte ein Wasser.` | neuter object stays same |
| `die Banane` | `Ich möchte eine Banane.` | feminine object stays same |
| `die Eier` | `Ich brauche Eier.` / `Ich brauche keine Eier.` | plural object keeps zero/`keine` |

## Accusative Adjective Mini-Table

| Base Idea | Object Phrase | Flashcard Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| `der Zug ist lang` | `Ich sehe einen langen Zug.` | Convert "The train is long" into "I see a long train." |
| `das Restaurant ist gut` | `Ich sehe ein gutes Restaurant.` | Convert "The restaurant is good" into "I see a good restaurant." |
| `die Stadt ist groß` | `Ich sehe eine große Stadt.` | Convert "The city is big" into "I see a big city." |
| `die Restaurants sind gut` | `Ich sehe gute Restaurants.` | Convert plural restaurants into an object phrase. |

## Word-Order And Negation Chunks

| Entry | Meaning / Function | Example | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Möchtest du ...?` | yes/no question with `möchten` | `Möchtest du eine Cola?` | finite verb must come first |
| `Trinkst du ...?` | yes/no question with `trinken` | `Trinkst du einen Tee?` | subject follows the verb |
| `Am Morgen ...` | time phrase in first position | `Am Morgen trinke ich Kaffee.` | verb still stays in second sentence position |
| `kein/keinen/keine` | no/not any noun | `Ich esse keinen Fisch.` | choose article by gender/case |
| `nicht gern` | not gladly / do not like doing | `Ich trinke nicht gern Tee.` | not the same as "no tea at all" |
| verb + `gern` | liking an activity | `Ich tanze gern.` | avoid `Ich mag tanzen` in this A1 pattern |

## Meal And Table Phrases

| Entry | Meaning | Example | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| `das Frühstück` | breakfast | `Ich frühstücke um acht Uhr.` | noun and verb forms differ |
| `das Mittagessen` | lunch | `Das Mittagessen ist gut.` | compound noun |
| `das Abendessen` | dinner | `Ich esse Abendessen.` | often use `zu Abend essen` later; keep A1 simple |
| `Guten Appetit!` | enjoy your meal | before eating | not a greeting |
| `Es schmeckt gut.` | it tastes good | meal evaluation | more specific than `Es ist gut.` |
| `Sonst noch etwas?` | anything else? | shop/server question | understand as chunk |
| `Das ist alles, danke.` | that is all, thanks | customer closing | closes the request politely |
