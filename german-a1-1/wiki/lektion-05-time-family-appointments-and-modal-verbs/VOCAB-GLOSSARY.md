# Vocabulary Glossary: Lektion 05 Time, Family, Appointments, And Modal Verbs

Source note: `lektion-05-time-family-appointments-and-modal-verbs.md`
Context file: `CONTEXT.md`
Processed: 2026-07-08
CEFR scope: A1.1 course-local vocabulary, enriched where the agenda identifies a topic but not a full lexical list.

## Level Summary

- Main output domain: time, family, appointments, availability, and simple obligation/ability/intention.
- Must-produce chunks: `Wie spät ist es?`, `um ... Uhr`, `von ... bis ...`, `mein/meine`, `Hast du Zeit?`, `Kann ich einen Termin haben?`, `Ich muss/kann/will ...`.
- Main trap: treating modal verbs like English word order; in German, the infinitive goes to the end.

## Fixed Phrases And Question Chunks

| Entry | Meaning | Example | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Wie spät ist es?` | What time is it? | `Wie spät ist es?` | memorize as a chunk |
| `Es ist ... Uhr.` | It is ... o'clock. | `Es ist zehn Uhr.` | include `Uhr` in formal written practice |
| `Wann ...?` | When ...? | `Wann treffen wir uns?` | asks point in time |
| `Wie lange ...?` | How long ...? | `Wie lange dauert der Kurs?` | asks duration |
| `Hast du Zeit?` | Do you have time? | `Hast du am Freitag Zeit?` | verb first in yes/no question |
| `Ich habe keine Zeit.` | I have no time. | `Ich habe am Freitag keine Zeit.` | `keine`, not `nicht` |
| `Kann ich einen Termin haben?` | Can I have an appointment? | `Kann ich einen Termin haben?` | polite service frame |

## Time Phrases

| Entry | Meaning | Example | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| `um ... Uhr` | at ... o'clock | `um 15 Uhr` | point in time |
| `am Montag/Freitag` | on Monday/Friday | `am Freitag` | `am` for weekdays |
| `nächste Woche` | next week | `Ich habe nächste Woche einen Termin.` | keep sentence complete |
| `von ... bis ...` | from ... to ... | `von 10 Uhr bis 12 Uhr` | duration, not point |
| `am Wochenende` | on the weekend | `Ich muss am Wochenende arbeiten.` | time phrase can be first or after verb |

## Family Nouns

| Entry | Meaning | Article / Plural | Example | Flashcard Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `die Familie` | family | `die Familien` | `Das ist meine Familie.` | Present your family. |
| `der Vater` | father | `die Väter` | `Mein Vater heißt Ali.` | Say "my father." |
| `die Mutter` | mother | `die Mütter` | `Meine Mutter wohnt in München.` | Say "my mother." |
| `der Bruder` | brother | `die Brüder` | `Ich habe einen Bruder.` | Say you have one brother. |
| `die Schwester` | sister | `die Schwestern` | `Ich habe eine Schwester.` | Say you have one sister. |
| `die Eltern` | parents | plural | `Meine Eltern wohnen in Ankara.` | Say "my parents." |

## Possessive Articles

| Entry | Function | Example | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mein` | my, masculine/neuter nominative | `mein Vater`, `mein Kind` | not `meine Vater` |
| `meine` | my, feminine/plural nominative | `meine Mutter`, `meine Eltern` | not `mein Mutter` |
| `dein/deine` | your, informal | `dein Bruder`, `deine Schwester` | match noun gender/number |
| `sein/seine` | his/its | `sein Vater`, `seine Mutter` | article ending changes |
| `ihr/ihre` | her/their | `ihr Bruder`, `ihre Eltern` | context disambiguates her/their |
| `Ihr/Ihre` | your, formal | `Ihr Termin`, `Ihre Familie` | capitalized formal `Sie` form |

## Appointment Nouns And Verbs

| Entry | Meaning | Example | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| `der Termin` | appointment | `Ich habe einen Termin.` | masculine object: `einen Termin` |
| `die Verabredung` | arrangement/date | `Ich habe eine Verabredung.` | not every appointment is service-related |
| `der Friseur` | hairdresser | `Beim Friseur` | person/place context |
| `Zeit haben` | to have time | `Hast du Zeit?` | split phrase: `Zeit` stays noun |
| `sich treffen` | to meet | `Wir treffen uns um 15 Uhr.` | include `uns` |
| `dauern` | to last | `Der Kurs dauert zwei Stunden.` | the event is subject |

## Modal Verbs

| Verb | Meaning | High-Yield Forms | Example | Flashcard Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `müssen` | must / have to | `ich muss`, `du musst`, `er/sie muss`, `wir müssen`, `Sie müssen` | `Ich muss arbeiten.` | Say you must work. |
| `können` | can / be able to | `ich kann`, `du kannst`, `er/sie kann`, `wir können`, `Sie können` | `Ich kann am Freitag ins Kino gehen.` | Say you can go to the cinema. |
| `wollen` | want to | `ich will`, `du willst`, `er/sie will`, `wir wollen`, `Sie wollen` | `Ich will schlafen.` | Say you want to sleep. |
