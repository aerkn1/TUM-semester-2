## Activity Phrases

### tanzen
- Meaning: to dance.
- Forms: `ich tanze`, `du tanzt`, `er/sie tanzt`, `Sie tanzen`.
- Example: `Ich tanze gern.`
- Pitfall: preference marker is `gern`, not an English-style auxiliary.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "I like dancing."
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### schwimmen
- Meaning: to swim.
- Forms: `ich schwimme`, `du schwimmst`, `er/sie schwimmt`, `Sie schwimmen`.
- Example: `Schwimmst du gern?`
- Pitfall: yes/no question starts with the finite verb.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask whether someone likes swimming.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### wandern
- Meaning: to hike.
- Forms: `ich wandere`, `du wanderst`, `er/sie wandert`, `Sie wandern`.
- Example: `Ich wandere nicht so gern.`
- Pitfall: `nicht so gern` is softer than `nicht gern`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "I do not really like hiking."
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### spazieren gehen
- Meaning: to go for a walk.
- Forms: `ich gehe spazieren`, `du gehst spazieren`, `er/sie geht spazieren`, `Sie gehen spazieren`.
- Example: `Peter geht mit Susanne spazieren.`
- Pitfall: sentence bracket: `geht` early, `spazieren` at the end.
- Flashcard prompt: Say that Peter goes for a walk with Susanne.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Fußball spielen
- Meaning: to play football/soccer.
- Forms: `ich spiele Fußball`, `du spielst Fußball`, `er/sie spielt Fußball`, `Sie spielen Fußball`.
- Example: `Ich spiele am Freitag Fußball.`
- Pitfall: `Fußball` often appears at the end after time information.
- Flashcard prompt: Say that you play football on Friday.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Schach spielen
- Meaning: to play chess.
- Forms: `ich spiele Schach`, `du spielst Schach`, `er/sie spielt Schach`, `Sie spielen Schach`.
- Example: `Ich spiele nicht so gern Schach.`
- Pitfall: no article in this activity phrase.
- Flashcard prompt: Say that you do not really like playing chess.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Fahrrad fahren
- Meaning: to ride a bike.
- Forms: `ich fahre Fahrrad`, `du fährst Fahrrad`, `er/sie fährt Fahrrad`, `Sie fahren Fahrrad`.
- Example: `Du fährst am Wochenende Fahrrad.`
- Pitfall: stem change in `du fährst`, `er/sie fährt`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say that you ride a bike on the weekend.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### ins Kino gehen
- Meaning: to go to the cinema.
- Forms: `ich gehe ins Kino`, `du gehst ins Kino`, `er/sie geht ins Kino`, `Sie gehen ins Kino`.
- Example: `Gehst du gern ins Kino?`
- Pitfall: yes/no question begins with `Gehst`.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask whether someone likes going to the cinema.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

## Preference Words And Chunks

### gern
- Meaning: like to; gladly.
- Type: adverb/preference marker.
- Example: `Ich tanze gern.`
- Pitfall: do not translate word-by-word as "gladly" in the English sentence.
- Flashcard prompt: Add `gern` to a hobby sentence.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### nicht gern
- Meaning: do not like to.
- Type: fixed preference chunk.
- Example: `Ich schwimme nicht gern.`
- Pitfall: not the same as `kein`; this negates liking, not a noun.
- Flashcard prompt: Say one hobby you do not like.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### nicht so gern
- Meaning: do not like so much.
- Type: softened preference chunk.
- Example: `Ich wandere nicht so gern.`
- Pitfall: weaker than `nicht gern`; use when you want a softer answer.
- Flashcard prompt: Soften a negative hobby answer.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Ja, gern.
- Meaning: yes, gladly.
- Type: acceptance chunk.
- Example: `Gehen wir ins Kino? Ja, gern.`
- Pitfall: use as short acceptance; for grammar practice, expand to a full sentence too.
- Flashcard prompt: Accept an invitation.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Nein, ich habe keine Zeit.
- Meaning: no, I do not have time.
- Type: refusal chunk.
- Example: `Nein, ich habe keine Zeit.`
- Pitfall: `Zeit` is negated with `keine` as a noun phrase.
- Flashcard prompt: Politely refuse an appointment.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

## Verbs

### machen
- Meaning: to do/make.
- Forms: `ich mache`, `du machst`, `er/sie macht`, `Sie machen`.
- Example: `Was machst du gern?`
- Pitfall: W-question order is `Was machst du`, not `Was du machst`.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask what someone likes doing.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### gehen
- Meaning: to go.
- Forms: `ich gehe`, `du gehst`, `er/sie geht`, `Sie gehen`.
- Example: `Gehst du gern ins Kino?`
- Pitfall: yes/no question starts with `Gehst`.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask whether someone is going to the cinema.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### spielen
- Meaning: to play.
- Forms: `ich spiele`, `du spielst`, `er/sie spielt`, `Sie spielen`.
- Example: `Ich spiele Fußball.`
- Pitfall: games/sports often do not need an article.
- Flashcard prompt: Say two things you play.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### treffen
- Meaning: to meet.
- Forms: `ich treffe`, `du triffst`, `er/sie trifft`, `Sie treffen`.
- Example: `Wann treffen wir uns?`
- Pitfall: `du triffst` has a vowel change; use cautiously if not yet drilled.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask when you meet.
- CEFR note: enriched from course phrase `eine Verabredung treffen`.

### sein
- Meaning: to be.
- Forms: `ich bin`, `du bist`, `er/sie ist`, `Sie sind`.
- Example: `Ich bin Student.`
- Pitfall: irregular; do not regularize it.
- Flashcard prompt: Say your role/job with `sein`.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### haben
- Meaning: to have.
- Forms: `ich habe`, `du hast`, `er/sie hat`, `Sie haben`.
- Example: `Ich habe am Freitag Zeit.`
- Pitfall: `du hast`, `er/sie hat`; not fully regular.
- Flashcard prompt: Say whether you have time on Friday.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

## Nouns And Noun Phrases

### das Hobby
- Meaning: hobby.
- Plural: `die Hobbys`.
- Example: `Meine Hobbys sind Tanzen und Schwimmen.`
- Pitfall: plural commonly uses `-s`.
- Flashcard prompt: Name two hobbies.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### der Freund / die Freundin
- Meaning: male friend / female friend; can also mean boyfriend/girlfriend by context.
- Plural: `die Freunde` / `die Freundinnen`.
- Example: `Meine Hobbys, meine Freunde.`
- Pitfall: relationship meaning depends on context.
- Flashcard prompt: Say one thing your friend likes.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### die Verabredung
- Meaning: appointment, arrangement to meet.
- Plural: `die Verabredungen`.
- Example: `Wir treffen eine Verabredung.`
- Pitfall: in everyday German, `eine Verabredung haben` is also common; the course phrase is `eine Verabredung treffen`.
- Flashcard prompt: Make a simple appointment sentence.
- CEFR note: course-local A1.1.

### der Beruf
- Meaning: profession, job.
- Plural: `die Berufe`.
- Example: `Was ist dein Beruf?`
- Pitfall: `Beruf` is not the same as a temporary hobby.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask someone's profession.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### der Journalist
- Meaning: male journalist.
- Plural: `die Journalisten`.
- Example: `Er ist Journalist.`
- Pitfall: profession after `sein` often appears without article in simple identity statements.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "He is a journalist."
- CEFR note: course-local A1.1.

### die Journalistin
- Meaning: female journalist.
- Plural: `die Journalistinnen`.
- Example: `Sie ist Journalistin.`
- Pitfall: feminine profession often adds `-in`; plural often `-innen`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "She is a journalist."
- CEFR note: course-local A1.1.

### der Kurs
- Meaning: course.
- Plural: `die Kurse`.
- Example: `Wählen Sie ein Kursangebot.`
- Pitfall: `Kurs` is masculine.
- Flashcard prompt: Choose a course offer.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### das Kursangebot
- Meaning: course offer.
- Plural: `die Kursangebote`.
- Example: `Ich wähle ein Kursangebot.`
- Pitfall: compound noun gender follows the final noun: `das Angebot`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say which course offer you choose.
- CEFR note: course-local A1.1.

### die Zahl
- Meaning: number.
- Plural: `die Zahlen`.
- Example: `Zahlen von 20 bis 1000.`
- Pitfall: number production is oral as much as written.
- Flashcard prompt: Say five numbers from 20 to 1000.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

## Time And Scheduling Words

| Item | Type | Meaning | Example | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `am Freitag` | time phrase | on Friday | `Ich spiele am Freitag Fußball.` | `am` = `an dem` |
| `am Wochenende` | time phrase | on the weekend | `Du fährst am Wochenende Fahrrad.` | time phrase sits in middle field |
| `wann` | question word | when | `Wann treffen wir uns?` | not `wenn` for a direct question |
| `Zeit` | noun, `die` | time | `Ich habe keine Zeit.` | negated as `keine Zeit` |

## Numbers 20-1000

| Item | Meaning | Example | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|
| `zwanzig` | 20 | `20` | base tens word |
| `einundzwanzig` | 21 | `21` | ones before tens |
| `dreißig` | 30 | `30` | uses `ß` |
| `hundert` | 100 | `100` | no article for simple counting |
| `tausend` | 1000 | `1000` | useful for large-number practice |
