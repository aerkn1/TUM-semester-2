### Yellow

- German noun capitalization and spelling need active repair: `Student`, `Freizeit`, `Schweiz`, `Universität`, `Französisch`, `Gitarre`.
- Verb conjugation was inconsistent: `hat`, `lacht`, `fährt`; avoid infinitives when a finite verb is required.
- English-to-German lexical swaps need attention: `guitar` -> `Gitarre`, `nature` was not a verb.
- Preposition fixed phrases need drill: `um 7 Uhr`, `zu Ende`, `ins Bett`, `auf dem Markt`.

### Red

- Sentence-role logic around objects and companion subjects needs repair: `trifft Freunde`, not `uns mit seine Freunde`; `Sie sehen zusammen einen Film` because the subject is plural in meaning.
- Verb choice by object needs repair: `trinkt einen Kaffee`, `kauft einen Kuchen`, `malt ein Bild`.

## Refined Mental Models

- If a blank follows a number, check plural: `2 Schwestern`.
- If a blank follows a modal/preposition/time phrase, decide whether the missing word must be a noun, verb, or preposition before guessing.
- For time:
  - clock time = `um`
  - weekday span = `von ... bis`
  - evening/weekend = `am`
  - months/season phrase = `in den Sommermonaten`, `im Winter`
- For movement and fixed places:
  - `nach Hause`
  - `ins Bett`
  - `auf dem Markt`
  - `bei Mercedes`
- For verbs, test the object:
  - `Musik hören`
  - `ein Buch lesen`
  - `Sport machen`
  - `Kaffee trinken`
  - `einen Kuchen kaufen`
  - `ein Bild malen`
  - `einen Film sehen`
