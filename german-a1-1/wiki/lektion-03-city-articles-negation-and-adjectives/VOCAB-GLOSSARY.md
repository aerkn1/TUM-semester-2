## Level Summary

- Main output domain: identify and describe city objects/people/places.
- Must-produce items: article grid, `kein` grid, adjective phrases, city questions.
- Main trap: confusing predicate adjectives (`Der Zug ist lang`) with attributive adjective endings (`ein langer Zug`).

## Articles And Determiners

### der
- Meaning: definite article for masculine nominative singular.
- Example: `Der Zug ist lang.`
- Pitfall: not interchangeable with biological gender; objects can be masculine.
- Flashcard prompt: Give a masculine noun with `der`.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### das
- Meaning: definite article for neuter nominative singular; also demonstrative pronoun in `Das ist...`.
- Example: `Das Restaurant ist gut.`
- Pitfall: `Das ist...` can introduce any gender: `Das ist eine Stadt.`
- Flashcard prompt: Give a neuter noun with `das`.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### die
- Meaning: definite article for feminine nominative singular and all nominative plurals.
- Example: `Die Stadt ist groß.` / `Die Restaurants sind gut.`
- Pitfall: singular feminine and plural share `die`; check noun number.
- Flashcard prompt: Give one feminine and one plural example with `die`.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### ein
- Meaning: indefinite article for masculine and neuter nominative singular.
- Example: `Das ist ein Zug.` / `Das ist ein Restaurant.`
- Pitfall: adjective ending differs: `ein langer Zug`, `ein gutes Restaurant`.
- Flashcard prompt: Use `ein` with one masculine and one neuter noun.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### eine
- Meaning: indefinite article for feminine nominative singular.
- Example: `Das ist eine Stadt.`
- Pitfall: do not use `ein Stadt`.
- Flashcard prompt: Use `eine` with `Stadt`.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### kein
- Meaning: negative article for masculine and neuter nominative singular.
- Example: `Das ist kein Zug.` / `Das ist kein Restaurant.`
- Pitfall: use `kein` for noun negation, not `nicht ein` as default.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "That is not a train."
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### keine
- Meaning: negative article for feminine nominative singular and plural.
- Example: `Das ist keine Stadt.` / `Das sind keine Restaurants.`
- Pitfall: plural negation uses `keine`, even though plural indefinite has zero article.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "Those are no restaurants."
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

## Nouns And Noun Phrases

### der Zug
- Meaning: train.
- Plural: `die Züge`.
- Example: `Das ist ein langer Zug.`
- Pitfall: adjective before noun: `langer`, not `lang`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a long train.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1 from course source.

### der Mann
- Meaning: man.
- Plural: `die Männer`.
- Example: `Das ist ein sportlicher Mann.`
- Pitfall: plural has umlaut: `Männer`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a sporty man.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### das Restaurant
- Meaning: restaurant.
- Plural: `die Restaurants`.
- Example: `Das ist ein gutes Restaurant.`
- Pitfall: plural indefinite has no article: `gute Restaurants`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a good restaurant.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### das Kind
- Meaning: child.
- Plural: `die Kinder`.
- Example: `Das ist ein kleines Kind.`
- Pitfall: plural is `Kinder`, not `Kinds`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a small child.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### die Stadt
- Meaning: city.
- Plural: `die Städte`.
- Example: `Hamburg ist eine große Stadt.`
- Pitfall: feminine singular uses `eine` and adjective `-e`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "Hamburg is a big city."
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### die Frau
- Meaning: woman; Mrs./Ms. in address.
- Plural: `die Frauen`.
- Example: `Das ist eine aktive Frau.`
- Pitfall: do not use `Frau` for every female person in all contexts; it can be adult woman or title.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe an active woman.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### das Hotel
- Meaning: hotel.
- Plural: `die Hotels`.
- Example: `Das ist ein modernes Hotel.`
- Pitfall: neuter adjective after `ein` uses `-es`.
- Flashcard prompt: Say "That is a modern hotel."
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1 from board example.

### das Kino
- Meaning: cinema.
- Plural: `die Kinos`.
- Example: `Gehst du ins Kino?`
- Pitfall: motion phrase is `ins Kino`, not just `in Kino`.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask whether someone goes to the cinema.
- CEFR note: connected from Lektion 2 and city/free-time use.

### der Bahnhof
- Meaning: train station.
- Plural: `die Bahnhöfe`.
- Example: `Wo ist der Bahnhof?`
- Pitfall: compound contains `Bahn` + `Hof`; plural has umlaut.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask where the train station is.
- CEFR note: enriched A1 city/direction vocabulary.

### die Jahreszeit
- Meaning: season.
- Plural: `die Jahreszeiten`.
- Example: `Welche Jahreszeit ist das?`
- Pitfall: not the same as clock/time `Zeit`.
- Flashcard prompt: Name a season.
- CEFR note: course-local from agenda.

## Adjectives

### lang
- Meaning: long.
- Attributive examples: `ein langer Zug`, `lange Züge`.
- Predicate example: `Der Zug ist lang.`
- Pitfall: no ending after `ist`; ending before noun.
- Flashcard prompt: Contrast `Der Zug ist lang` and `ein langer Zug`.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### sportlich
- Meaning: sporty, athletic.
- Attributive examples: `ein sportlicher Mann`, `sportliche Männer`.
- Predicate example: `Der Mann ist sportlich.`
- Pitfall: masculine after `ein` uses `-er`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a sporty man.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### gut
- Meaning: good.
- Attributive examples: `ein gutes Restaurant`, `gute Restaurants`.
- Predicate example: `Das Restaurant ist gut.`
- Pitfall: `ein gutes`, not `ein gut`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a good restaurant.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### klein
- Meaning: small.
- Attributive examples: `ein kleines Kind`, `kleine Kinder`.
- Predicate example: `Das Kind ist klein.`
- Pitfall: neuter after `ein` uses `-es`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a small child.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### groß
- Meaning: big, large.
- Attributive examples: `eine große Stadt`, `große Städte`.
- Predicate example: `Die Stadt ist groß.`
- Pitfall: keep `ß`; adjective ending still attaches: `große`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a big city.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### aktiv
- Meaning: active.
- Attributive examples: `eine aktive Frau`, `aktive Frauen`.
- Predicate example: `Die Frau ist aktiv.`
- Pitfall: final `-e` in attributive feminine/plural forms.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe an active woman.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### modern
- Meaning: modern.
- Attributive examples: `ein modernes Hotel`.
- Predicate example: `Das Hotel ist modern.`
- Pitfall: neuter after `ein` uses `-es`.
- Flashcard prompt: Describe a modern hotel.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1 from board example.

## Verbs And Question Chunks

### sein
- Meaning: to be.
- Forms: `ich bin`, `du bist`, `er/sie/es ist`, `Sie sind`.
- Example: `Das ist ein Zug.`
- Pitfall: article/adjective decisions often appear after `sein`.
- Flashcard prompt: Identify an object with `Das ist...`.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### gehen
- Meaning: to go.
- Forms: `ich gehe`, `du gehst`, `er/sie geht`, `Sie gehen`.
- Example: `Gehst du ins Kino?`
- Pitfall: yes/no question starts with the finite verb.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask if someone is going somewhere.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### kommen
- Meaning: to come.
- Forms: `ich komme`, `du kommst`, `er/sie kommt`, `Sie kommen`.
- Example: `Kommst du aus München?`
- Pitfall: `kommen` can ask origin or actual coming; context decides.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask whether someone is coming.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Was ist das?
- Meaning: What is that? for a thing.
- Type: question chunk.
- Example: `Was ist das? Das ist ein Restaurant.`
- Pitfall: use `wer` for people, `was` for things.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask what an object is.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Wer ist das?
- Meaning: Who is that? for a person.
- Type: question chunk.
- Example: `Wer ist das? Das ist Frau Weber.`
- Pitfall: do not use for objects.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask who a person is.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Was sind das?
- Meaning: What are those? for plural things.
- Type: question chunk.
- Example: `Was sind das? Das sind Restaurants.`
- Pitfall: plural question uses `sind`.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask what multiple objects are.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Wo ist ...?
- Meaning: Where is ...?
- Type: direction/location chunk.
- Example: `Wo ist der Bahnhof?`
- Pitfall: `wo` asks location; `woher` asks origin.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask where the station is.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.

### Wann ...?
- Meaning: when?
- Type: question word.
- Example: `Wann kommst du?`
- Pitfall: do not use `wenn` for this direct A1 question.
- Flashcard prompt: Ask when someone is coming.
- CEFR note: inferred A1.1.
