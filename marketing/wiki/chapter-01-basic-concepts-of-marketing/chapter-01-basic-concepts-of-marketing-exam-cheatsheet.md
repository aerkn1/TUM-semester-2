# Chapter 01: Basic Concepts Of Marketing - Exam Cheatsheet

Source note: [chapter-01-basic-concepts-of-marketing.md](chapter-01-basic-concepts-of-marketing.md)

Created: 2026-07-15

Exam context: Marketing exam on 2026-07-22 is a 90-minute written exam. Prepare short applied answers, not only recognition. This sheet does not replace the full note and does not mark `First Pass` as completed.

## Core Exam Move

```text
Customer need -> target group -> value proposition -> 4Ps -> customer response
-> retention/recovery -> economic consequence -> trap.
```

Use Chapter 01 as the language layer for all later chapters. If a case asks about brand, product, price, promotion, or place, start by identifying the customer problem and target group.

## Must-Know Definitions

| Concept | Exam-safe wording | Use when asked |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | Management of value-creating exchange relationships with selected target customers. | "Marketing is more than advertising." |
| Customer value | Perceived benefit/problem solution relative to alternatives and sacrifice. | Why customers choose or stay. |
| Marketing triangle | Customer needs, own offering, competitor offerings. | Diagnose competitive advantage. |
| Sustainable competitive advantage | Advantage that is lasting, substantial, and noticeable to customers. | Avoid feature-list answers. |
| 4Ps | Product, Price, Promotion, Place. | Operative tools after strategy. |
| 3Rs | Recruitment, Retention, Recovery. | Relationship phase logic. |
| Segmentation | Dividing the market into identifiable, profitable, reachable groups. | Targeting and positioning questions. |
| Customer satisfaction | Emotional response from comparing expectations with perceived performance. | Service, complaint, retention cases. |

## High-Yield Chains

Customer-value chain:

```text
Customer value -> acquisition -> satisfaction -> retention -> economic success
```

Marketing management process:

```text
Situation analysis -> forecast -> goals -> strategy -> 4Ps
-> implementation -> controlling
```

Satisfaction rule:

```text
Expectations > performance -> dissatisfaction
Expectations = performance -> confirmation
Expectations < performance -> high satisfaction
```

## 4Ps And Relationship Phases

| 4P | Recruitment | Retention | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | clear value, branding, packaging | service quality, differentiation, warranties | fixes, innovation, individual service |
| Price | introductory offers, discounts | fair price-performance, guarantees, bundles | compensation, special conditions |
| Promotion | awareness, direct mail, social media | clubs, newsletters, engagement | personal apology, service communication |
| Place | availability, POS, direct sales | convenient delivery, regular visits | key account support, exclusive access |

Exam sentence:

```text
A good marketing plan does not stop at acquisition; it also retains customers and recovers relationships after failures.
```

## Consumer Behavior Router

| Case signal | Use this concept | Exam implication |
|---|---|---|
| Frequent low-risk purchase | Convenience good, habitual buying | Availability and cues matter. |
| Comparison before purchase | Shopping good | Price-performance and information matter. |
| High identity or budget impact | Specialty good | Trust, differentiation, brand meaning matter. |
| Quality visible before buying | Search property | Specs and comparison work. |
| Quality known after use | Experience property | Reviews, trials, guarantees help. |
| Quality hard to verify even after use | Trust property | Reputation and credibility dominate. |
| Strong information search | Cognitive involvement | Provide diagnostics and evidence. |
| Strong emotion/impulse | Emotional involvement | Design, placement, and affect matter. |

## Written-Exam Answer Skeletons

Marketing is more than advertising:

```text
Marketing starts from selected customer needs, creates value through product,
price, promotion, and place, manages relationships through recruitment,
retention, and recovery, and turns customer value into long-term economic success.
Advertising is only one promotion instrument.
```

Marketing triangle case:

```text
1. Customer need/wish: ...
2. Own offering: ...
3. Competitor offering: ...
4. Advantage: lasting? substantial? noticeable?
5. Decision: strengthen the 4P that creates the missing customer value.
```

Complaint recovery case:

```text
Original failure -> complaint type -> recovery response
-> avoid double deviation -> restore trust/retention.
```

Segmentation case:

```text
Segment basis -> identifiable? -> profitable? -> reachable?
-> suitable target? -> matching 4P decision.
```

## Traps And Corrections

| Trap | Correction |
|---|---|
| Marketing = advertising. | Advertising is one promotion instrument inside the 4Ps. |
| A feature is automatically an advantage. | It must matter to customers and differ from competitors. |
| Satisfaction means loyalty. | Satisfaction supports retention but does not guarantee it. |
| Switching barriers are always good. | Positive barriers can help; negative lock-in can harm trust and welfare. |
| Segmentation is just demographics. | Include profitability, reachability, behavior, needs, and strategy fit. |
| 4Ps are the strategy. | The 4Ps operationalize strategy after analysis and goal setting. |

## Last Line

```text
Marketing answer = customer need + target choice + value creation + 4P execution + relationship outcome.
```
