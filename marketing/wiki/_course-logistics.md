Study implication:

- Treat lecture slides and in-class discussed papers as examinable.

- Build exam answers around definitions, frameworks, examples, and managerial implications.

## Learning Goals

- Learn core elements of the marketing management process.
- Learn theories, models, and strategies for building brand equity.
- Explain and apply goals and instruments of product policy and new product development.
- Explain and apply characteristics and elements of pricing policy.
- Explain and apply characteristics and elements of communication policy and social media marketing.
- Explain distribution system design.
- Use marketing research methods to make informed marketing decisions.
