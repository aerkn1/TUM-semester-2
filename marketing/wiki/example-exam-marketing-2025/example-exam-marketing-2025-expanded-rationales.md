## Question 11 - Price-Cut Volume Increase

Correct answer: **B**

Calculation:

```text
Old price = EUR 200
Variable cost = EUR 100
Old contribution = 200 - 100 = EUR 100

New price after 10% cut = EUR 180
New contribution = 180 - 100 = EUR 80

Required multiplier = old contribution / new contribution
Required multiplier = 100 / 80 = 1.25
Required volume increase = 25%
```

Why B is correct: a 25% volume increase keeps total contribution constant.

Why the other choices are not: a 10% answer confuses price reduction with required volume increase. Any smaller increase fails to replace lost contribution per unit. Any much larger answer overcompensates beyond the break-even volume requirement.

## Question 16 - Expected-Profit Pricing

Correct answer: **A**

Calculation:

```text
Variable cost = EUR 30

p1 = EUR 50
Contribution = 50 - 30 = EUR 20
Profit if competitor price is EUR 50 = 20 * 4,000 = EUR 80,000
Profit if competitor price is EUR 60 = 20 * 5,000 = EUR 100,000
Expected profit = 0.5 * 80,000 + 0.5 * 100,000 = EUR 90,000

p2 = EUR 60
Contribution = 60 - 30 = EUR 30
Profit if competitor price is EUR 50 = 30 * 2,000 = EUR 60,000
Profit if competitor price is EUR 60 = 30 * 3,500 = EUR 105,000
Expected profit = 0.5 * 60,000 + 0.5 * 105,000 = EUR 82,500
```

Why A is correct: `p1 = EUR 50` gives the higher expected profit, EUR 90,000.

Why the other choices are not: choices favoring `p2` overvalue the higher margin and undervalue lost quantity. Choices with other profit amounts usually skip probability weighting or multiply by the wrong scenario quantities.

## Question 22 - Bundling Revenue

Correct answer: **B**

Willingness-to-pay table:

| Customer | WTP A | WTP B | Bundle WTP |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | EUR 100 | EUR 40 | EUR 140 |
| 2 | EUR 60 | EUR 100 | EUR 160 |
| 3 | EUR 90 | EUR 90 | EUR 180 |

Option comparison:

- A: separate pricing `A = EUR 90`, `B = EUR 90`. A buyers are customers 1 and 3; B buyers are customers 2 and 3. Revenue = `2 * 90 + 2 * 90 = EUR 360`.
- B: pure bundle at `EUR 130`. All three customers buy. Revenue = `3 * 130 = EUR 390`.
- C: mixed bundle with bundle `EUR 150`, A `EUR 100`, B `EUR 100`. Customer 1 buys A, customer 2 buys B, customer 3 buys the bundle. Revenue = `100 + 100 + 150 = EUR 350`.
- D: pure bundle at `EUR 180`. Only customer 3 buys. Revenue = `EUR 180`.

Why B is correct: EUR 390 is the highest revenue.

Why the other choices are not: A misses some surplus but beats C. C is not automatically best just because it is mixed bundling. D sets the bundle price too high and loses two buyers.
