Error types:

- `memory`: could not recall a definition, rule, formula, framework, or step.
- `concept`: understood words but missed the mechanism or intuition.
- `application`: knew the idea but applied it to the wrong situation.

- `comparison`: confused similar frameworks, theories, legal tests, or metrics.
- `exam-writing`: answer was correct but poorly structured or not exam-ready.
