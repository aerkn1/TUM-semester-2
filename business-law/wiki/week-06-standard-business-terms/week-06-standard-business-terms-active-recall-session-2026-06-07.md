## Refined Mental Models

| Concept | Refined Mental Model | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| SBT route | Contract first, exact clause second: classify -> negotiation -> incorporation -> surprise -> ambiguity -> content control -> consequence. | green |
| SBT classification | Pre-formulated, repeated-use wording supplied by one party; defect or unfairness facts do not create SBT status. | yellow-green |
| Surprise vs ambiguity vs harshness | Hidden or unexpected = surprise; unclear = ambiguity; clear but unfair = content control. | yellow-green |
| Individual agreement | Real influence over wording removes that clause from SBT status; accept-or-leave does not. | green after repair |
| B2C vs B2B | Classify the actor by the purpose of this transaction, not by permanent identity. | green after clarification |
| B2B scrutiny | Section 307 is central; Section 309 is not directly applied in the standard B2B route but can influence Section 307 through Section 310 I. | yellow-green |
| Section 306 | Failed clause out; contract usually survives; statutory law fills the gap. | green |
| Battle of forms | Conflicting clauses can both fall out; neither side automatically wins. | green |
