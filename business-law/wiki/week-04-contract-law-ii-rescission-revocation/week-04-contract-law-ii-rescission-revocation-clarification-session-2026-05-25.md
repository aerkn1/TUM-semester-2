## Clarification Questions And Answers

### Question 1: Contract Law I Versus Contract Law II

User question: "what is the differences between contract law I and II in terms of evaluation and the contents? Is it like continuation pattern or evaluates the cases from different perspectives?"

Professor explanation:

```text
Contract Law I: Did a valid contract arise?
Contract Law II: Can a party attack or unwind the legal situation?
```

Contract Law I evaluates formation and validity:

- offer
- acceptance
- effectiveness and receipt
- interpretation
- missing form
- statutory prohibition
- public policy/usury
- standard-term control

Contract Law II evaluates exit routes:

- **Rescission** attacks a flawed declaration of intent.
- **Revocation** unwinds a valid reciprocal contract because performance went wrong.

Refined mental model:

```text
Contract Law I builds the house.
Rescission attacks a defective foundation stone.
Revocation returns the furniture after the valid house cannot be used as promised.
```
