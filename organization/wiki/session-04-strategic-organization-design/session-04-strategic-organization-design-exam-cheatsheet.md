# Session 04: Strategic Organization Design - Exam Cheat Sheet

Source note: [session-04-strategic-organization-design.md](session-04-strategic-organization-design.md)

Created: 2026-07-15

Use this sheet for rapid exam recall. It does not replace the full note and does not mark `First Pass` as completed.

## Core Exam Move

```text
Name strategic intent -> identify design tension -> compare alternatives
-> apply a test/framework -> state tradeoff and implementation risk.
```

## Strategy Definition

Strategy is an internally developed guiding concept aligned with capabilities, aimed at competitive advantage, and weighing opportunities and risks.

Exam-safe sentence:

```text
Strategy gives organization design a direction: it tells designers which capabilities, activities, tradeoffs, and outcomes matter.
```

## Strategy And Structure

| Logic | Meaning | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Structure follows strategy | intent is formed, then roles, incentives, and processes are designed to implement it | planned strategic redesign |
| Strategy follows structure | existing routines, power, capabilities, and structures shape what strategy becomes possible | strategy emerges or implementation changes intent |
| Strategy as practice | strategy is situated activity produced through social, material, and discursive work | middle managers, meetings, tools, interpretation matter |

Realized strategy can diverge from intended strategy because actors translate, negotiate, and adapt plans.

## Strategic Intent

| Term | Question | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Mission | What do we do? | present organizational task |
| Vision | What do we want to become? | future image |
| Purpose | Why do we exist? | broader meaning and justification |
| Goals | What do we aim to achieve? | concrete targets for design/action |

Trap:

```text
Mission, vision, purpose, and goals are not interchangeable.
```

## Three Strategic Tensions

### 1. Exploration Vs Exploitation

| Ambidexterity type | Meaning | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structural | separate units for exploration and exploitation | protects routine efficiency and experimentation | learning may not transfer to core |
| Contextual | same units/people balance both | embeds exploration in everyday work | time conflict and role ambiguity |

### 2. Focus Vs Diversification

Diversification creates value only if it passes Porter's tests:

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| Attractiveness | Is the target industry attractive or improvable? |
| Cost-of-entry | Does entry cost leave future profit? |
| Better-off | Does common ownership create synergy? |

Exam-safe line:

```text
Growth alone is not value creation; the better-off test asks whether ownership creates synergy.
```

### 3. Performance Vs Responsibility

| View | Core claim |
|---|---|
| Shareholder view | business primarily maximizes returns to owners within competition rules |
| Stakeholder view | responsibility extends to affected groups and long-term legitimacy |

Ethics is broader than legality:

```text
An organization may act legally and still create ethical, ecological, or social harm.
```

## Global Design Mini-Router

| Global integration | Local responsiveness | Likely design |
|---|---|---|
| high | low | global product structure |
| low | high | global geographic/multidomestic |
| high | high | global matrix |

Matrix answer:

```text
Useful for product x country demands, but creates dual authority and conflict complexity.
```

## Trap Corrections

| Trap | Correction |
|---|---|
| "Strategy is only top management plan." | Add strategy-as-practice and emergent strategy. |
| "Diversification means growth." | Apply attractiveness, cost-of-entry, better-off. |
| "Ethical equals legal." | Legal compliance is the minimum, not full responsibility. |
| "Ambidexterity means do both somehow." | Specify structural or contextual design. |

## Last Line

```text
Strategic design is the art of making structure, capabilities, incentives, and responsibility fit strategic intent while managing unavoidable tensions.
```
