# Session 06: AI And Organization - Exam Cheat Sheet

Source note: [session-06-ai-and-organization.md](session-06-ai-and-organization.md)

Created: 2026-07-15

Use this sheet for rapid exam recall. It does not replace the full note and does not mark `First Pass` as completed.

## Core Exam Move

```text
Define AI -> reject determinism -> choose effect domain
-> apply to tasks/roles/change/ethics -> propose governance rule.
```

## Definition And No-Determinism Sentence

```text
AI refers to machines performing cognitive functions usually associated with human minds, such as learning, interacting, and problem solving.
```

```text
AI effects are not technologically determined; they depend on development, introduction, interpretation, routines, roles, power relations, and governance.
```

## Four Effect Domains

| Domain | Ask in a case |
|---|---|
| Task division | Which tasks are automated, augmented, split, or integrated? |
| Roles, knowledge, expertise | Who trains, explains, sustains, learns, or loses expertise? |
| Stability and change | Does AI enable adaptation or reinforce old assumptions? |
| Fairness, ethics, accountability | Who is responsible, monitored, advantaged, or harmed? |

## Task Division Essentials

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Automation | machine takes over a human task |
| Augmentation | human and machine work together |
| Automation-augmentation paradox | automation creates adjacent human work; augmentation can prepare automation |
| Centaur | human and AI do different task parts |
| Cyborg | human and AI co-produce throughout the task |

Trap:

```text
Automation and augmentation are interdependent, not clean opposites.
```

## Roles And Expertise

Humans assist machines:

- training
- explaining
- sustaining/responsible use

Machines assist humans:

- amplifying analytical or creative capabilities
- interacting with customers/users/employees
- embodying physical capabilities through robotics

Expertise growth if AI provides feedback, variants, and learning opportunities. Expertise erosion if AI removes practice, separates production from interpretation, or creates dependence on outputs.

## Stability And Change

| Mechanism | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Bounded retheorization | small model adjustments without questioning assumptions |
| Sedimentation | old assumptions embedded in data/model |
| Capacitating | AI enables new actions/relations |
| Reframing | AI helps actors see routines differently |
| Shielding | AI blocks influences that could change action |
| Adhering | AI enforces established sequences |

## Ethics And Algorithmic Control

Generative AI risks:

- multipurpose usability
- unpredictable plausibility
- hyper-personalizability

Algorithmic control can monitor, allocate, evaluate, compare, reward, sanction, or restrict work.

Risk areas:

```text
bias, privacy, transparency, accountability.
```

Governance sentence:

```text
Machines should challenge humans, and humans should challenge machines.
```

## Aurelia Case Router

| Case angle | Diagnosis | Best recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Task division | NOVA reduces some work but creates validation/data/engineering work | redesign work allocation before broad scaling |
| Roles/expertise | senior productivity rises but junior learning and Sales Ops roles may erode or become invisible | protect learning routines and formalize data stewardship |
| Stability/change | historical scores may privilege established OEM patterns | review low-score/high-potential anomalies separately |
| Fairness/accountability | scores shape customer treatment and employee visibility | explainable factors, human owner, review rights, monitoring governance |

## Trap Corrections

| Trap | Correction |
|---|---|
| "AI is good/bad automatically." | Analyze organizational practice and governance. |
| "AI replaces humans." | Name automation, augmentation, and adjacent human work. |
| "Dashboard is neutral." | It may become algorithmic control. |
| Generic ethics answer | Give operating rules: owner, review rights, data limits, validation status. |

## Last Line

```text
AI exam answers score when they show what is redistributed: work, expertise, learning, accountability, and control.
```
