## Exam Information

Source: `organization/raw/00_TUM_Organization_Introduction.pdf`

- Exam language: English.
- Duration: 60 minutes.
- Scope: everything covered in the course is relevant, including the guest lecture.
- Materials: lecture slides are uploaded to Moodle.
- Questions relate both to conceptual content and to applied exercises.
- The exam tests understanding, application, and transfer.
- Exercise parts usually have no single perfect solution, but the course provides exercise solutions on Moodle.

Example question types from the lecture:

- Name and explain the definitional criteria of an organization.
- Assess whether `Fridays for Future` is an organization according to this definition.

Study implication:

- Prepare definitions plus application to cases.
- Build compare/contrast answers, especially for alternative organizational arrangements and contingency logic.
- Preserve exercise/case logic in topic notes because applied exercises are examinable.

## Learning Goals

- Explain central organization theory concepts such as structure, coordination, incentives, routines, culture, and change.
- Use concepts to analyze organizational problems, cases, and design choices.
- Compare alternative organizational arrangements and assess strengths, limits, and contingencies.
- Connect concepts to empirical cases and current developments such as AI, agility, and platforms.
- Develop precise, evidence-based arguments about organization.
