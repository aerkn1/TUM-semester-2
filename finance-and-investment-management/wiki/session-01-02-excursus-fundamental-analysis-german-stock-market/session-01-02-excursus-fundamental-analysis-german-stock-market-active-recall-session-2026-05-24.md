## Outcome

The user correctly understood that low M/B alone does not make a stock attractive. F-Score and red-flag signals are needed to distinguish a possible value opportunity from a value trap.

## Key Feedback

- M/B uses `Market Value of Equity / Book Value of Equity`.
- Company B in Case 1 was `600/400 = 1.5`, not `0.667`.
- If two firms both have M/B of 0.5, F-Score changes the interpretation: high F-Score can support a value opportunity; low F-Score plus negative operating signals suggests a value trap.
