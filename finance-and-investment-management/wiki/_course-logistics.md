## Course Structure And Exam Direction

The course consists of two independent but exam-relevant tracks:

- Lecture in Corporate Finance: financial statement analysis, investment analysis, capital budgeting, cost of capital, capital structure, capital structure and taxes.
- Exercise on Mathematical Basics: interests, annuities, redemptions, bonds, stocks, options.

Important structural note from the course introduction: the exercise content is independent of the lecture, meaning the mathematical-basics exercises do not build directly on the corporate-finance lecture sequence. For exam preparation, however, both must be practiced.

## Study Implications

- Because the exam is 100% written final exam and all lectures/exercises count, both conceptual interpretation and calculation fluency matter.
- The formulary reduces formula memorization pressure but does not remove the need to recognize the correct formula, map variables, handle timing, and interpret the result.
- Highest-value early sequence: financial statement analysis, interest calculation, annuities, investment analysis, redemptions, bonds.
