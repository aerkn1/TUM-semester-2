EOQ versus EPQ

The cost trade-off is related; the inventory geometry is different.

EOQ: instant replenishment

Inventory jumps to Q, then falls at demand rate lambda. The average inventory is Q/2.

Q* = sqrt(2 K lambda / h)

EPQ: gradual production

Demand consumes units while production runs. Inventory builds at p - lambda and peaks below Q.

Q* = EOQ x sqrt(p / (p - lambda))
